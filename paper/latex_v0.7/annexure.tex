\subsection*{C. Declaration}
This draft records our reported contributions and discloses the assistance used. The 35 included works were reviewed with AI assistance at the sections and versions recorded in the repository. We have not yet completed and documented human reading and independent verification of all included works. This statement does not certify completion of those tasks; the final submission declaration and signatures remain pending.

Use of AI tools: ChatGPT with SciSpace was used for the pilot search. Claude (Anthropic) verified metadata at arXiv and Crossref, ran further searches, screened abstracts, read the stated sections of the included works, extracted the matrix, and drafted the paper. ChatGPT independently checked selected numeric claims and the F6 utility coding, corrected the W021 comparison and F6 count, and regenerated draft v0.6.
